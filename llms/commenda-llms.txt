# Commenda

> Commenda is the Global Business Engine — AI software to incorporate and manage global entities, filings, and tax across 70+ countries. Built for international finance teams, Commenda integrates with your ERP to handle incorporation, entity management, indirect tax (VAT/GST/sales tax), corporate tax filings, and transfer pricing documentation.

Commenda serves finance teams, tax teams, controllers, legal teams, and accounting firms at startups through enterprise companies operating internationally. The platform connects to 100+ ERP and accounting integrations and is backed by local experts in every jurisdiction.

## Products

- [Entity Management & Incorporation](https://www.commenda.io/entity-management): Incorporate, manage, and wind down entities across 70+ countries. Includes compliance calendars, corporate governance, and local expert support.
- [Indirect Tax Suite](https://www.commenda.io/indirect-tax): Global VAT, GST, and sales tax compliance. Calculate tax obligations in 70+ countries, manage registrations, and automate filings. Integrates with major ERPs.
- [Transfer Pricing Suite](https://www.commenda.io/transfer-pricing): Transfer pricing documentation automation — local files, master files, and country-by-country reporting. Covers SMB through enterprise.
- [Corporate Tax & Financial Reporting Suite](https://www.commenda.io/tax-accounting): Corporate tax filings, compliance calendar management, and consolidated group reporting across all jurisdictions.
- [Integrations](https://www.commenda.io/integrations): Connect to 100+ business tools including NetSuite, QuickBooks, Xero, Shopify, Stripe, and more.
- [Commenda API](https://docs.commenda.io/engine/api-introduction): Embed Commenda's compliance infrastructure directly into your own product.
